About Our Brand

Just Tea Leaves started with a simple idea: tea should taste like the place it came from, not like the inside of a warehouse. We're a small team of obsessive steepers who source whole-leaf tea and get out of its way.

Every pot deserves leaves worth the wait.

Our story began in a cramped apartment kitchen, where two friends kept mailing each other tea samples from every trip they took. What started as a hobby turned into a habit, and the habit turned into a question we couldn't shake: why did so much of the tea sold nearby taste like dust and cardboard, when the good stuff was so easy to find if you knew where to look?

So we started looking. We built relationships directly with small family farms and cooperatives that still pick and dry tea the slow way, batch by batch, season by season. No shortcuts, no filler leaves swept off the factory floor, no mystery blends. If a batch doesn't taste the way it should, we don't sell it — we send it back and try again the next season.

That same care follows the leaves all the way to your cup. We pack in small runs, seal everything to keep the aroma locked in, and print the harvest date right on the bag so you always know how fresh your tea actually is. It's a little more work on our end. We think it's worth it.

Today, Just Tea Leaves ships whole-leaf black, green, herbal, and hibiscus blends to kitchens that care about the difference between a tea bag and an actual leaf. We're still a small operation, and we like it that way — it means every order gets packed by someone who genuinely loves this stuff.

What We Believe

Slow Sourcing

We work directly with a handful of small farms instead of buying from bulk auction lots, so every batch can be traced back to the people who grew it.

Whole Leaf, Always

No dust, no fannings, no mystery fillers. If it isn't a full, intact leaf, it doesn't go in the bag.

Honest Labeling

Harvest dates, origin, and steep times are printed clearly on every bag — no vague marketing language standing in for real information.
